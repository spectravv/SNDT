# SNDT v1.0.4

Bug fixes and small features after v1.0.3 (v1.1 shelved meanwhile).


## Fixes
- Messages at the bottom no longer pile on top of each other: a new message replaces the one still showing (e.g. switching Free → Grid placement quickly; reported by Kentōyäm).
- Lines that run straight through a station keep their lane on both sides more often (fewer sideways nudges at stations):
  - a bundle that leaves a station straight and only bends a little further on now counts as carrying straight on, so its lines are lined up across the station (it used to count as a turn and was left unaligned: 9中東綫 at 水枯圍, 8那烙綫 at 白場圍 and 法拉 on the Tung Sha map);
  - where two lines swap sides across a station, the side that can actually be reordered is reordered (a side that is one shared segment can't be, and used to "win" at no cost and change nothing: 7新田綫/9中東綫 at 莫古斯).
  - Layout check: rmp-tung-sha 22 → 9 sideways jumps, untitled-network 35 → 30, rmp-older 2 → 1 (baseline not saved yet).
- Where a line turns at a station and its piece ends in a rounded bend, the two pieces now meet in one corner (looking a little way back along the bend) instead of a small sideways step (13東環綫 at 水枯圍, 7新田綫 at 莎諾). Lines carrying straight on are left alone.
- Where lines still swap sides across a station, the order inside a shared segment can now change too (e.g. a line shared by the segments on both sides has to run in their middle); kept only if fewer lines cross at the segment's two ends. This also runs at stations with a hand-set line order, which only matters where that order can't be kept (馬麗口's order puts 3東西綫 between 9中東綫 and 13東環綫, which share a segment).
- Lines that have to swap sides (user decision 2026-10-31, "clean X on the segment"): when reordering a shared segment would fix the crossing at one station but cause one at its other end, the segment now keeps its own line order at each end and the lines cross once along it, at 45° in the middle of its longest straight stretch, with the usual crossing gap (swapPiece, t.endOrd). Before, they swapped inside the station's pill. Tung Sha map: blue 3東西綫 and purple 13東環綫 now cross between 當惠村 and 馬麗口 and run straight through 馬麗口 in their own lanes. Straight-through jumps on that map: 0.
- (Tried and reverted 2026-10-31: drawing slightly out-of-line segments dead straight by sliding the odd station's end (ef3331e). On the newer map 馬麗口 got pulled two ways by its segments to 黃海石 and 羅方 and its pill stretched; to be redone properly by the lines-first lane layout.)
- Lane layout switch (This map > Lines): "Stations first" (the layout so far, still the default) or "Lines first (beta)" (settings.layout 'lines', user decision 2026-10-31 to keep both). Lines first, step 2 of the plan:
  - a segment whose stations are up to one lane out of line, and which otherwise runs along one axis, is laid on that axis; the gap goes to the lane solver as a "run straight" rule (corrB) instead of being drawn as a jog;
  - all lane shifts are solved together (least squares reweighted towards least absolute error, light pull to the station points), then every rule it nearly met is made exact, so leftovers are only clean steps where it chose to break a rule.
  - Layout check, lines first vs stations first: Curt's Tung Sha maps jumps 5/4/5 → 2/2/4, jogs 10/14/12 → 9/12/8; rmp-tung-sha jogs 3 → 0, jumps 6 → 5. Slightly worse so far: rmp-older (one small straight step, one stretched mark) and rmp-tung-sha random variations (6.5 → 7.4 jumps); tuning is step 5. Stations first draws exactly as before.
- Lines first, polishing (2026-10-31):
  - across a station, bundles line up on the shift most shared lines agree on, so the rest carry straight on and only the odd one steps (was: the average, so all stepped a little; lines test map 4 → 1 steps at Station 2, rmp-tung-sha 5 → 3 jumps, its random variations 7.4 → 6.3);
  - a straight piece that ends at a bend point which only ends a line can move with its lanes (rmp-older 花木路: no more step and stretched mark);
  - labels, click areas and the one-way chevron follow the drawn mark (markAt) instead of the station point.
  - Layout check now leaves curved, arc, ring and hand-drawn segments out of the jog count.
- Lines first is the default for NEW maps (blank, example, Rail Map Painter and MTR imports; user decision 2026-10-31). Maps saved before it existed load as Stations first (normalize marks a missing settings.layout as 'stations'), and the switch now stores 'stations' explicitly. Layout check: lines-first baselines saved in test-maps/baseline-lines, and --mode lines is now its default. New UI strings ("Lane layout", "Stations first", "Lines first (beta)", the explanation) are not on Crowdin yet.
- Lane layout is now a main option (user decision 2026-10-31): a LINES | STATIONS switch in the top bar right after GRID | FREE (follows the open map, a message explains each mode when switched). To make room, the top bar's black switches get narrower from 1399 px, the map name and language picker from 1299 px; GRID | FREE still hides below 1200 px, LINES | STATIONS below 1100 px (the option stays in This map > Lines for narrow screens and phones). No header overflow from 1100 px up; the old overflow around 950 px shrank from 39 to 12 px.
- Lane layout switch only in the top bar now (removed from This map > Lines); on phones it sits in the ☰ menu under "Lane layout". Top bar order is now GRID | FREE, LINES | STATIONS, SIMPLE | ADVANCED, MAP | TIMELINE (Simple/Advanced and Map/Timeline swapped, also in the phone menu). Between 901 and 1099 px wide the switch is hidden for lack of room (no other way to switch there yet).

## Player ideas
- Grid dots stay readable when zoomed out: every 2nd/4th/... dot is drawn so they stay at least 6 px apart, and below 25% the grid is hidden (idea by Curt). Snapping still uses the real grid.
- Double-clicking a track (or "Select the whole line") now selects the line with all its stations and bend points, so it can be moved or added to the timeline in one go (idea by Curt). The right panel still shows the line's settings and Delete still deletes just the line. The timeline's "Add selection" now also takes a whole line, as its stations and segments from one end to the other (it used to ignore it).
- Text objects: the font picker is now in Simple mode too (it was Advanced only), next to a new Italic switch (idea by Paddy). Not done: his Cianan Ur font (SIL OFL, not on Google Fonts, so SNDT would have to host the font file and embed it in exports; for the user to decide).
- Facility icons beside station names: Labels > "Facility icons" Top / Centre / Bottom (default Top, as before) and "Icons per row" (0 = all in one row, as before); extra icons wrap onto more rows (idea by Curt).
- Legend: "Items per column" (0 = one column, as before) wraps a long legend into side-by-side columns; a section heading never ends a column (idea by Curt).
- Isolated line view: a "Facilities" switch in the strip's bar shows each station's facility icons under it, below any transfer badges, wrapped so they fit between stations; included in PNG/SVG export (idea by Curt). Remembered in this browser like the name style.
- New font "Cianan Ur" (Cianán Ùr by Feòrag, SIL Open Font License 1.1, requested by Paddy) for labels and text objects. SNDT ships the original OTF in site/fonts/ with its OFL and FONTLOG; the browser only fetches it when a map uses it. PNG/SVG exports, the isolated line view export and timeline videos embed it (LOCAL_FONTS, localFontCSS), so it shows up outside SNDT too. Preview: publish fonts/cianan-ur.otf alongside the page.
- Station numbers (idea by Aquaqua, drawn like Singapore's MRT codes): a line gets a "Station code" (e.g. NS), a first number and "Number from the other end" in its panel; its stations are numbered along it (bend points and pass-through stops skipped). This map > Stations > "Station numbers: Codes as station marks" draws each numbered station as its codes in the lines' colours, side by side at interchanges (JS 1 | NS 4 | BP 1); labels move clear of them; a station not in service shows them faded instead of crossed out. A station's number on a line can be typed in its panel (the automatic one shows as a hint). Off by default.
- Layout check baselines re-saved (lines first: all maps; stations first: Curt's maps): the only change was the legend rows now drawn inside their own groups (same picture).
- Isolated line view: the Facilities switch is now a bordered button with a tick box and a facility icon right after the name options (it looked like plain text before; user feedback).
- Station number codes can be sized by hand (user request): This map > Stations > "Code size" (slider plus a number box; empty = automatic from the station size) and "Code size here" in a station's panel (empty = same as the map). The text scales with the badge.
- Station number codes are sized by hand on the map (user request, "like district areas but always a rectangle"): select a numbered station and drag one of the eight square handles around its codes; corners change width and height, edges one of them; the box stays centred on the station (Alt for half steps); the badges share the width and the text shrinks to fit. "Reset code size" in the station's panel undoes it. The per-station size slider is gone (the map-wide "Code size" stays as the starting size).
- Station number codes of a station not in service are now striped (diagonal stripes in the background colour under the text, which keeps a thin outline in the line colour) instead of faded (user request).
- Station number codes in a box sized narrow (but tall enough) put the letters above the number (EC over 2) instead of shrinking them on one line; each badge picks whichever layout gives bigger text (user request).
