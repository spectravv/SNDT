# SNDT v1.0.2

- Timeline: "Reverse drawing direction" in the Line group panel (click a line's coloured bar on the track). Flips that line's cards so it draws from its other end (A-B-C-D-E becomes E-D-C-B-A); press again to flip back. Remembered per line in `timeline.rev`, so Generate from lines keeps the direction. (idea: the user)
- Round circle lines: a loop line has "Draw as a round circle" in its Stops pane. Ticking it moves the stations onto the circle fitted through them (least-squares; undoable), and every segment of the line then follows that circle in the loop's direction, overriding any bend type the segment had (e.g. from an import). Lines sharing those segments run alongside as rings. A station dragged off the circle later bends the line smoothly through it; "Put the stations on the circle" snaps them back. Stored as `line.circle`. (idea: the user)
- Deleting a segment: with a segment selected (click on a line), Delete removes only that link between the two stations instead of the whole line. A loop opens there; otherwise the line splits in two and the second part keeps the look but is left out of the legend (if the first part disappears, the second takes its place in the legend). The segment pane has "Delete this segment" and "Select the whole line" buttons. Delete on a whole-line selection still deletes the line. (idea: the user)
- Double-click on a line selects the whole line (it used to add a stop there; that is still in the right-click menu and the segment pane). (idea: the user)
- Map cleaner: "Clean up map…" under the map name in the Map panel (also in search). Lists lines with fewer than two stations, duplicate lines (same stops as an earlier line, also reversed or, for loops, started at another stop; the first one stays), bend points no line uses and stations on no line (those start unticked). Only the ticked items are removed, in one undo step. (idea: rthorpey12)
- Isolated line view, "ILVF": the strip button next to + in the Lines pane picks one or more lines and shows each as a straight horizontal strip in a panel at the bottom of the map, for platform displays and station signs. Stations are evenly spaced with names above (second names and `sub` in smaller italics; end stations bold), interchanges are ringed dots with short stubs in the other lines' colours and a small name tag per line under them, A→Z (transfer groups count), stations not in service get a cross. Names: Auto (angled when they don't fit), Straight or Angled. Own PNG (2×) and SVG export (`<map>-strip`). A slider under the strip (and mouse drag) scrolls it sideways; drag the bar on top of the panel to resize it (remembered per browser, double-click resets). Loop lines end back at their first station (user OK). Follows the map's colours and label font; redraws as the map changes. (idea: Paddy)
