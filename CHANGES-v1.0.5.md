# SNDT v1.0.5

## Fixes
- Lines first: where a hand-drawn segment has a bend point right beside its station (a short hook into the station point), the line now runs straight past the station and the station is drawn on it, instead of stepping sideways there (7新田綫 at 麻灣, 8那烙綫 at 豐各 on Curt's Tung Sha maps). Layout check: turning steps 2/2/3 → 0/0/1. Yellow 9中東綫 now shows a step at 利立 instead, where it runs back over itself (see the 利立 bug in ROADMAP). Stations first is unchanged.
- A line that passes a station without stopping ("Stops here" unticked) now runs in the outer lane there, beside the station mark, instead of keeping a lane through it (user decision 2026-11-02, option B of the Ma Lai Hau mockups). It goes to the side where fewer lines have to cross over, and this beats the station's dot order; a hand-set order on the segment still wins. Both layouts. At 馬麗口 on Curt's map, purple shares a segment with yellow on one side and with blue on the other, so yellow and blue now swap at the station.
- Lines that turn together at a station are only lined up across it when they stay (nearly) side by side; where two or more other lines come between them on one side, they are left alone. Before, both were pushed far sideways so one of them lined up, which drew a crook into the station (cyan and magenta at Ivybridge Market on the c&t Lake Network map). Both layouts; the other test maps draw the same.
- Reordering lines at stations (dragging a line's dot onto another) works again where it silently did nothing (reported 2026-11-02 on the c&t Lake Network map):
  - a line order set on a segment with its ↑/↓ arrows kept overriding the dots forever; swapping now clears those orders along the stretch the two lines run together;
  - a swap now holds along that whole stretch: older dot orders at the other stations on it are swapped too, so the newest choice wins;
  - lines meeting a station from different directions were measured on mismatched arms, so the saved order didn't match the drawing;
  - a swap the layout can't draw (e.g. a line between two lines that share the next segment) is refused with a message instead of saving an order that draws something else.
  On that map 40 of 43 neighbour swaps now work in Lines first (the rest are refused with the message), before most did nothing.
- A segment's hand-set line order only counts while it includes all the segment's lines. One saved before another line was added used to push the new line to the far side, so it crossed the whole bundle (the Ivybridge Line at Shenton Lane and Sai Wan East).
- Station numbers: "Renumber stations" in a line's Station code settings writes the numbers in order along the line into every station (replacing hand-typed ones), so adding an infill station no longer means retyping every station after it (player idea 2026-11-02).
- Striped lines (and lines under construction): the light blocks are drawn one by one along the line and turned with it, so they stay square and evenly spaced around bends instead of coming out skewed (player report 2026-11-02).
- Isolated line view, big interchanges (player report 2026-11-02, Chrome and the app): the other lines' stubs are spaced further apart and each is outlined in the background colour, so two lines of the same colour side by side no longer merge; a station with 3+ other lines is drawn as a pill over its stubs instead of a circle they poke out of.
- Isolated line view: interchange style switch Names / Codes / Dots (player idea 2026-11-02). Names is the London-style boxes as before; Codes shows a coloured badge per line with its code (the line's station code prefix, else the initials of its name without "Line", e.g. Chiselsmith & City Line → CC), wrapping into rows; Dots shows only the stubs. Remembered per browser; exports follow it.
- Fare zones as a kind of area (player idea 2026-11-02, cosmetic like districts): select an area and set Kind to Fare zone. It is drawn like the Tube map's zones: a plain shaded area with no border (grey by default) and its number, big and faint, near two opposite corners so an inner zone over the middle doesn't hide it. Stack zones in alternating shades; "Number size" sets the number's size.
- Station codes as marks: a code box made taller by hand stacks the codes in rows (A B C D E F → A B C / D E F → A B / C D / E F → one per row), using whichever number of rows makes the text biggest; a short last row gets wider badges. The corner rounding follows one badge, not the whole box.
- Areas can be joined again (the old "add a rectangle" join only worked for rectangles and had lost its button): select two or more districts or fare zones and press "Join into one area". They become one area with one outline, keeping the look of the first one selected; it moves and selects as one. "Split into separate areas" in its panel undoes it.
- Fare zone numbers are placed where the whole number fits inside the zone and isn't covered by a zone drawn over it (nearest the top left, plus nearest the bottom right when that is elsewhere), so odd shapes no longer push the number outside.
- Joined areas: every part keeps its own corner points (drag to move, click a midpoint to add one, double-click to remove), not only the first area's; a joined rectangle becomes a four-corner free shape so it can be edited the same way.
- Fare zones: "How many numbers" (0–12, default 2) next to the zone number, with Number size beside it (both shown in Simple mode too). The first number sits nearest the top left and each next one in the free spot farthest from the others, so they spread over the zone; numbers that would crowd each other are left out.
- Fare zones in a bright colour (yellow, light green…) draw their numbers darker and stronger, so they stay readable on the light shade.
