# SNDT v1.0.6

## New
- Manual layout: a third button in the lane layout switch (LINES | STATIONS | MANUAL). Picking Manual freezes the lanes exactly as they are drawn at that moment (from Lines first or Stations first) and saves each line's sideways offset at both ends of every segment, in lanes (`seg.lanes {lineId:[start,end]}`, plus a third value for the legs in between on routes with three or more legs; `settings.layoutBase` remembers which automatic layout was frozen). The automatic layout no longer moves frozen lines. Segments drawn after freezing follow the automatic layout until they are moved or reset.
  - Lanes are kept per part: each leg of a segment's route between corners has its own value (`seg.lanes {lineId:[part1,part2,…]}`; a straight or smooth segment keeps `[start,end]` and steps across at 45° in the middle when they differ).
  - Click a line to pick the part under the pointer (between two corners); Shift-click picks more parts, on any segment or line, and Shift-clicking a picked part drops it. Drag sideways to move every picked part: each part moves across its own direction, so dragging both parts of an L diagonally moves its corner. Corners follow to where the parts meet, also at bend points between segments. Steps are half a lane on the grid, a tenth of a lane in Free placement, and any amount with Alt. On touch, a part has to be picked before it can be dragged, so swiping across lines still pans. (User request 2026-11-12, after a mockup on c&t Lake.)
  - In Manual, hand-drawn routes don't show the "add a bend" handles in the middle of each leg, where grabbing a line now moves its lanes; the corner handles stay.
  - Several picked parts show as "N parts of lines" in the panel.
  - The segment panel shows "Lanes on this segment": each line's offset per part (or at both stations), typed in lanes, plus "Back to the automatic lanes here", which re-freezes just that segment from the automatic layout. Line order on the segment is hidden in Manual, because it only affects the automatic layout.
  - Station marks follow their lines in Manual, as they do in Lines first.
  - Leaving Manual drops the saved lanes and goes back to the automatic layout (undo brings them back).
  - Freezing is faithful: on the test maps the frozen drawing matches the automatic one to within half a pixel, apart from two odd hand-drawn hooks on c&t Lake.
- Header: the switches (Grid/Free, Lines/Stations/Manual, Simple/Advanced, Map/Timeline), Help and Export are now an icon with a small name below it, so the bar takes much less room (user request 2026-11-12); every control in the bar (map name, search, icon buttons, language, undo/redo) is now the same 40px height as those; GRID | FREE and the lane layout switch hide at the old widths again (below 1200 / 1100px). The Discord button shows the Discord logo and sits right next to the search box (VoltedFromMatrix asked for a Discord link in the empty bottom-left corner; the user preferred next to the search).
  - While parts of lines are picked, every other line fades to 15% opacity, so the lines being edited stand out; a line with a picked part stays whole. Stations not on those lines fade with them, labels included. Editor only, never in exports (user request 2026-11-12: first a 10% toggle, then made automatic).
- Merge lines (user request 2026-11-12): with two or more lines selected (Shift-click them on the map), the Selection panel has "Merge into one line". The lines are chained end to end at the stations where one ends and the next begins, turning a line round where needed (its one-way settings flip with it); a chain that comes back to its start becomes a loop. The merged line keeps the name, colour and settings of the first line selected, plus the per-segment colour/style/status of the others. Everything else that pointed at the merged lines (segment line orders, manual lanes, station line orders, timeline) now points at the kept line. Lines that don't meet end to end, or loop lines, are refused with a message saying which. Tested: splitting a line in two and merging the halves gives back the same stop list with no leftover references.
- Lines list: Shift-click (or Ctrl-click) a line to add it to the selection or take it out, e.g. to merge lines (user request 2026-11-12).
- Manual edits are no longer lost when switching to Lines first or Stations first (user report 2026-11-12). Leaving Manual drops only the lanes that are still where the automatic layout puts them; lanes moved by hand stay and keep applying in the other layouts, while everything else follows the automatic layout as usual. The segment panel says so for such a segment, with "Back to the automatic lanes here". Going back to Manual keeps those hand-moved lanes and freezes the rest.
- Station labels drag freely (Advanced): the label no longer jumps between the automatic sides while dragging. An automatic label is fixed to the side it is shown on when the drag starts (keeping any push away from other labels), then its anchor point snaps to the grid in Grid placement (Alt for 1-unit steps) and moves in tenths in Free placement (user request 2026-11-12).
- Station labels: "Text alignment" (Auto / Left / Centre / Right) in the station panel, right under the name, side by side with Label position (left column: position, right column: alignment, to save space; user's request), for one or several selected stations (user request 2026-11-12). The label stays where it is; its rows (name lines and the subtitle) line up left, centred or right inside it. Auto keeps the old behaviour (aligned towards the station side). Saved as `station.lalign`.
- Station marks can be sized freely, like the station code boxes (user request 2026-11-12): with one station selected, drag the square handles around its mark (corners or edges; both sides move, so the mark stays centred and turned along its lines; Alt for half steps), or type "Mark length" and "Mark thickness" in the station panel (empty = automatic). "Reset mark size" goes back to automatic. Saved as `station.markW` / `markH`. Works for every mark drawn as a dot, pill or shape (stops, interchanges, hubs, terminus, bus, train, HSR, portal and the custom shapes); not for bend points, local dots, arrows, tick stops on one line, custom icons, or codes used as the mark (those have their own handles). Labels keep clear of a sized mark.
- Labels can be dragged on their own in Simple mode too (before, only in Advanced; in Simple, dragging a label moved its station). Drag the station's mark to move the station (user report 2026-11-12).
- Fix: one-way chevrons on stations showed at two-way stops shared with a one-way branch (Curt, Yelloway/Spawn). A stop is now marked one-way only when every line stopping there runs one way through it, all in the same direction.
- Fix: a fare zone with a tiny number size (1) and a huge count (200) froze the app (Curt). The number placement now tries at most about 80 × 80 spots, and the count is capped at 12 like the field says.
- Fix: the tutorial came back after every refresh for players who closed the tab in the middle of it (Absolutely Nobody; seen on Firefox, Linux Mint). It now counts as seen as soon as it opens by itself; Help opens it again.
- Fix: with the gap between areas set to 0, an area's outline ran on beside the outline of an area drawn over it (Curt, "Outlines on districts overlap each other"). The outline is now cut a little wider than the fill, so it stops at the other area's outline.
- Fonts (This map › Labels, Advanced), from player ideas (StarlightPixels: Helvetica; Curt: separate fonts for CJK and secondary text; "or maybe just add an option to add your own fonts"):
  - Helvetica in the font list: it uses the computer's own Helvetica, with Arimo (free, same letter widths) as the stand-in, which is also what exports carry.
  - Main font, plus "CJK text" (Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters in names) and "Subtitles" (the second line under a name, e.g. the English name under the Chinese one), each "Same as main" by default. E.g. Hong Kong MTR style: CJK Noto Serif HK, main/subtitles Noto Sans HK.
  - Add a font: any Google Fonts family by its name (checked against Google Fonts; saved in the map as `settings.gfonts`).
  - Upload a font file (.ttf, .otf, .woff, .woff2, up to 8 MB): checked by loading it, then saved inside the map (`M.fonts`), so it travels with the map; the panel says to upload only fonts whose licence allows that. Added fonts can be removed (anything using them goes back to the default).
  - Exports (PNG, SVG, isolated line strip, timeline video) carry the fonts inside: uploaded ones whole, Google ones cut down to the letters the map uses (a map with Figtree, Noto Serif HK and Arimo: about 200 KB). Before, PNG exports didn't carry Google fonts at all.
