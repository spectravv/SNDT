Terms

Last updated 21 November 2026

SNDT is free to use. The editor is open source, your maps are yours, and anything you contribute stays credited to you. This page explains the few rules that come with that.

Using SNDT

You can use SNDT on sndt.cc for anything you like, including maps for commercial work, without paying or asking. SNDT is offered as it is: it's made with care, but there's no guarantee it is free of bugs or that the site is always online, so keep your own copies (export) of maps that matter to you.

Your maps

Maps you make belong to you. SNDT doesn't claim any rights to them. When you share a map with a link or put it in the gallery, you let SNDT store and show it (with a picture and your nickname) so others can open it, until you make it private again or delete it. See the privacy page for what is stored.

Gallery rules

  • Only share maps you made or have the right to share.
  • No hateful, sexual, violent or harassing content, and no personal information about other people.
  • Don't use official logos or present a map as an official map of a real transit operator.

Maps that break these rules can be taken out of the gallery, and accounts that keep breaking them can be closed.

The licence

SNDT's editor is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The code is on GitHub. Fonts that come with SNDT are under their own free licences (SIL Open Font License), kept next to the font files.

SNDT's author also uses the same code in other versions of SNDT, including paid ones (such as a planned Steam version), which may not be open source. The editor on sndt.cc stays free.

Contributing translations and code

By sending a contribution (a translation on Crowdin, a pull request or a patch), you agree that it's your own work, that it is published under the AGPL-3.0 like the rest of SNDT, and that SNDT's author may also use, change and distribute it in any version of SNDT, under any licence, including paid and closed-source ones. You keep the copyright, and translators are credited in the app.

The full wording is in CONTRIBUTING.md. If you translated before these terms were added, you'll be asked for your OK before your work is used outside the free version.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the change will be announced in SNDT's release notes and on Discord. Questions? Ask on the SNDT Discord.
