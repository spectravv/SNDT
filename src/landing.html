From a sketch to a real map.

Click stations, click them again in order, and the line draws itself. SNDT keeps the parallel lines tidy, the labels out of the way and the corners at clean angles, so you can think about the network instead of the drawing.

01

Lines that behave.

Where lines share a track they run side by side, keep their order through every station and cross cleanly when they have to.

  • Lines first lane layout keeps through-running lines dead straight
  • Interchanges drawn as one mark, with walking links between nearby stations
  • Grid or free placement, 45° angles or smooth curves
02

Play it back as a film.

The timeline builds your network station by station, with camera moves, line boards and an end legend, then exports it as a video.

03

Look down one line.

The isolated line view straightens any line into the strip you see above train doors, with station codes, connections and facilities. Export it as PNG or SVG.

On a phone

Fits in your pocket.

Pan with one finger, double-tap to select, long-press for the menu. Add SNDT to your home screen and it opens like an app, even without a connection.

Made with Minecraft servers

Running a server? It speaks blocks.

SNDT grew up drawing server networks, so it knows the details: dye colours, route lengths in blocks, travel times by minecart or ice boat, teleport commands, and an importer for MTR mod servers.

Made with its players.

Most of SNDT's features started as an idea from someone drawing their own network. Bring yours.
