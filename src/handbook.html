SNDT Handbook · v1.0

Draw your server's transit map

SNDT is a free editor for transit maps of Minecraft servers: stations, lines, rivers, parks and districts, drawn on a grid and exported as images, backups or animated videos. This handbook explains every tool and setting, in both the Map and the Timeline sections, with live demos you can play with right here.

Simple and AdvancedSNDT has two control levels, switched in the header. Settings marked Advanced only show in Advanced mode; everything else is in both. The search bar switches to Advanced for you when you pick an Advanced setting.

Your first map

  1. Press S (or pick Add station in the tool bar) and click the map wherever a station should go. Stations snap to the grid.
  2. Press L for the line tool and click your stations in order. Clicking empty map adds a new stop on the way. Press Enter when the line is done.
  3. Press V to select and move things. Click a station to rename it in the panel on the right.
  4. Export your map from Export: a PNG or SVG image, or a JSON backup.
Draw a lineline tool is on

Click the map to place stops; each click adds the next station. Press Enter to finish, then click a station or track to select it.

Your work saves automatically in your browser. The maps menu (the map's name in the header) starts new maps and switches between them; see Maps and saving.

The screen

Header

  • Maps menu (the map's name): new map, duplicate, switch, import. See Maps and saving.
  • Search bar: finds any tool, action or setting. See Search.
  • Support SNDT (heart) and Discord: the Ko-fi page and the SNDT Discord server.
  • Live room: edit a map together. See Live rooms.
  • Help: the tutorial and this handbook.
  • Language (11 languages) and theme (auto, light, dark).
  • Undo and redo, Map / Timeline, Simple / Advanced, and Export.

Around the map

  • Tool bar (top left): Select, Station, Line, and one button that holds the other drawing tools (it shows the last one you used). See Tools.
  • Lists (left panel): every line and station. Click one to select it, use the eye to hide a line or a whole group, and type to find a station. Drag the panel edge to resize it; double-click the edge to reset.
  • Inspector (right panel): the settings of whatever is selected, or the map's own settings when nothing is. Sections fold open and closed by their headings.
  • Zoom bar (bottom right): Bends shows or hides bend points, − / + zoom, the percentage shows the zoom, and Fit fits the whole map.
  • Coordinates (bottom left): where the cursor is on the map.

On phones the panels become drawers and a bottom sheet; see Phones and the app.

Selecting

  • Click to select; Shift+click adds to the selection; drag on empty map draws a selection box (hold Shift to add).
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection by one grid cell. Alt+drag moves without snapping.
  • Del deletes, Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+A selects every station.
  • Scroll to zoom; Shift+scroll pans sideways; F fits the map.

Stations

Select a station to edit it. The panel has the sections below.

Station Simple

  • Name. In Advanced, press Enter in the name for a second line.
  • Type: Station, Local stop (a small dot), Interchange, Hub (double ring), Terminus, Rail station, HSR station, Tram stop (a tick), Bus stop, To another city (an arrow off the end of the line) or Bend point (no marker, the line just turns there).
  • Shape (Station, Interchange and Terminus): Same as map, or one of Circles, Ticks (London style), Squares, Rounded squares, Diamonds and Bars. A station on several lines side by side stretches its shape across all of them. The map-wide shape is in Map → Stations.
  • Label position: the compass puts the name on one of eight sides, × hides it, and Auto: find free space picks the side with the most room. A side you pick stays on that side; if it would sit on a line it only moves further out.
  • Facilities: Airport, Ferry, Cable car, Bus, Rail, Metro, Tram, Parking and Landmark icons next to the name.
  • Custom icon: see Custom icons.
  • Not in service: draws a cross over the station and greys its name.
  • Served by: the lines through the station; click one to select it.
Station shapesmap-wide shape

Position Advanced

Map X and Map Y place the station exactly. Follow in-game X/Z keeps it where its in-game coordinates say, and Move to in-game location moves it there once (both use Game coordinates).

Label style Advanced

Sublabel adds a second, smaller name (for example the name in another language). Text colour, Coloured background (with its colour), Label nudge X, Label nudge Y, Label rotation °, Label size and Weight (Automatic, Bold, Regular) style the name. You can also drag a label on the map in Advanced.

In-game location Advanced

Dimension (Overworld, Nether, The End), X, Y, Z and Notes. With coordinates set, the panel shows the matching spot in the other dimension and a teleport command to copy, and line segments between two stations with coordinates show their distance and travel time.

Fine control Advanced

Marker size overrides the map's station size for this station. Below the sections: Duplicate, Remove from lines and Delete station.

Several stations at once

Select several stations to get Set type for all, Facilities for all, Custom icon, Label side for all and Line up: Same row, Same column, On a 45° line, Even spacing ↔, Even spacing ↕, plus Mark as not in service and Mark as in service.

Out-of-station transfers

With two or more stations selected, Link as out-of-station transfer joins them with a walking link, for interchanges between stations that aren't in the same building.

Custom icons

Give a station your own picture, such as a company logo or an in-game item. In the station's panel, Custom icon → + Upload PNG or SVG….

  • Replace marker draws the icon instead of the station's marker; On top of marker keeps the marker and puts the icon over it.
  • Size scales the icon from 25% to 400%. Labels move out to make room.
  • Uploaded icons stay in the map's icon library: other stations pick them with one click, without uploading again. Remove unused icons clears out icons no station uses.
  • Select several stations to give them all the same icon.
Small filesPNG and other pictures are shrunk to 160 pixels at most, and small SVGs are kept as they are, so maps stay quick to save and share.
Custom iconwith the station panel

The selected station's panel is on the right. Upload any PNG or SVG from your device, then try both modes and the size slider. Nothing here is saved.

Lines

Drawing and extending

  • With the line tool, click stations in order; empty map adds a stop, a track adds a stop on that track. Enter (or Done on phones) finishes.
  • To extend a line, select it and use Extend from start or Extend from end, click its first or last station with the line tool, or use Auto build.
  • Double-click a track (long-press on phones) to insert a station; a segment's panel also has Insert station and Insert bend point.

Line Simple

  • Name and Legend text (what the legend shows instead of the name).
  • Colour · Minecraft dyes: the 16 dye colours, or a Custom colour.
  • Line type: Subway / metro, Tram / light rail, Bus, Regional rail, Local rail, High-speed rail, Ferry, Minecart rail, Ice boat road, Boat on water, Walkway, Elytra route or Other. The type picks a matching style and travel speed, and groups lines in the lists and the legend.
  • Line style: Solid, Narrow, Dashed, Dotted, Railway (dashed centre), Striped, Double (hollow), Outlined or Thin dashed.
  • Status, whole line: Open, Planned or Under construction.
  • Leave out of the legend.

Stops

The stops in order, with buttons to move each one earlier or later or remove it. Loop back to the first stop makes a circle line; Reverse flips the order.

Fine control Advanced

Width, Opacity, Speed, blocks per second (for travel times and the Timeline), Custom dash pattern, and Draw above / Draw below to decide which line passes over at a crossing. Below: Duplicate line and Delete line.

One segment of a line

Click a track to select just that segment. For the line on that segment you can set its own status (Same as line, Open, Planned, Building), Colour here and Style here (Reset colour and style here undoes both), and the Direction.

Bends

Every segment between two stations bends one way. Set the bend for one segment in its panel (Bend for this segment), or for the whole map in Map → Lines.

BendShape
Straight, then 45°Runs straight first, then finishes at 45°.
45°, then straightStarts at 45° and finishes straight.
DirectOne straight line between the stations, at any angle.
Across, then turnA right angle: across first, then up or down.
Turn, then acrossA right angle: up or down first, then across.
CurveA smooth S-curve.
ZigzagStraight, then 45°, then straight again, with the straight part split evenly between both ends.
Bend typesdrag a station

Free shape

With a segment selected, drag the handles on the track to give it any shape: the dots in the middle of each piece add a new point, and points can get curve handles. Smooth rounds the whole shape, and the panel can clear the points again.

Corners

Corner rounding (Map → Lines) rounds every corner: Sharp, Soft, Round, Wide or Sweeping. In Advanced, Corner radius sets an exact radius for the map, Corner radius on this segment for one segment, and a bend point has its own Corner radius at this bend point.

Mix types

In Map → Lines, switch Bend types from One type to Mix types and tick the types you want. Every segment that has no bend of its own then gets the ticked type that fits best:

  • Direct when the two stations already line up straight or at 45°, unless that would cross several other lines.
  • Otherwise the bend that keeps lines smoothest through their stations and crosses the fewest other lines.
  • Curve only where nothing else fits, more often for stations far apart.

Selecting a segment shows which type was picked; choosing a type there fixes it for that segment. Keep at least two types ticked, or switch back to One type.

Mix typesmove stations and watch

Bend points

A bend point lets a line turn without a station: set a station's type to Bend point, or use Insert bend point. The Bends button (or B) shows or hides them while you edit; they never show in exports.

One-way segments

Select a segment; the line's part of the panel has Direction: Two way, or one way in either direction (named by its two stations). Chevrons along the track show the way. A stop where every segment of a line is one-way gets a small arrow in its marker, so passengers can see it is only served in one direction.

Inserting a station in a one-way segment keeps both halves one-way, and Reverse on the line keeps the arrows pointing the same way on the map.

One waywith the segment panel

The middle segment is selected. Use the Direction buttons in the panel, or click another segment first.

Lines side by side

Lines that share track run side by side on their own, and stay in the same order through stations so they don't swap places. Line width and Gap between lines (Map → Lines) set how far apart they are.

Reorder lines at stations Advanced

Turn on Reorder lines at stations (in Map → Lines or in a station's panel), then select a station: a dot sits on each line's track just outside the station, in lane order. Drag one dot onto another to swap the two lines there. Reset to automatic undoes it. While reordering is on, Auto build is hidden so the dots and stubs don't overlap. A segment's panel also has Line order on this segment.

Reorder linesdrag a dot onto another

Where lines cross

Where two lines cross, the one higher in the Lines list passes over. Where lines cross: Draw straight over, Small gap, Medium gap or Large gap cut into the line underneath; in Advanced, Gap size (0 draws straight over) sets it exactly. Change which line is on top with Draw above / Draw below in a line's Advanced settings.

Auto build

Select a station and short stubs point to where you could build next, three grid steps long. A floating bar below the station picks + New line or Extend … for each line that ends there; extending is the default when a line ends at the station.

  • At the end of a line: three stubs, straight on and 45° either way.
  • Anywhere else, or for a new line: a stub in every free direction.
  • Click a stub to start the line tool from the station; the stub's tip is a temporary bend point that disappears when you place the next station, so that segment uses the normal bend.

Turn it off in Map → Lines → Auto build.

Map settings

Click empty map so nothing is selected; the panel shows the map's own settings.

Map

Map name, shown in the maps menu and used for exported files.

Colours

Background, Text & outlines, Station fill and Water, or a Colour template: Paper, Night, Blueprint, Sandstone, Slate, Mint, Mono, Overworld, Nether and The End. Templates change those four colours; line colours, parks and areas stay as they are.

Colour templatespick one

Lines

Bend types, Auto build, Corner rounding (and Corner radius Advanced), Reorder lines at stations, Where lines cross (and Gap size Advanced), Line width and Gap between lines Advanced.

Stations

Single-line stops: the shape of every station that has no shape of its own (see Stations). Station size Advanced.

Labels

Label size, Label font (Overpass, Figtree, Pixelify Sans, Arial, Georgia, Courier New), Label halo (an outline in the background colour so names stay readable over lines), Keep station names off lines, Bigger Chinese, Japanese and Korean text (with CJK text size, times the normal size in Advanced) and Bold names at interchanges.

Grid

Grid size sets the snapping step; Snap all to grid moves everything onto it. Hold Alt to place freely.

Legend

Show legend. The legend lists your lines grouped by type. MTR imports with more than 20 lines start with it hidden. Drag it anywhere; click it for its Heading, its X and Y, and Hide legend.

Game coordinates

Blocks per grid cell links the map to your world, so in-game coordinates become map positions and distances. Nether ×8 treats Nether coordinates as eight times further. Place stations moves every station with coordinates to its in-game spot.

Shortcuts

The keyboard shortcuts; see Shortcuts.

Text, areas, water, nature and images

Text T

Click to place text. Text, Size, Colour, Rotation °, Align (Left, Centre, Right), X, Y, Font (Same as labels, or one of the label fonts) and Bold, plus Duplicate and Delete text.

Areas R

Drag to draw a district (the panel shows Draw an area while the tool is on). Select an area for Label, Colour, Fill strength, Lock in place (so clicks go through to what's on top), Dashed border, X, Y, Width, Height, Corner radius, Label size and Gap where areas meet. Turn into free shape lets you drag its corners into any outline; Delete area removes it.

Water W

Pick River to paint with a brush (Brush size), Fill shape to click a lake or sea outline, or Eraser. Water colour has presets (Map blue, Minecraft, Pale, Night). Smooth corners and Smooth curves round the shapes; Remove last stroke and Clear all water undo painting. Water's colour is also a map colour in Map → Colours.

Nature N

Click points around a shape and close it on the first point (or press Enter). With the tool on, Fill for new areas picks what you draw next; select an area for its Nature area panel. Fill: Park, Forest, Plains, Desert, Mesa, Snow, Mountain, Swamp or Cherry grove, or a Custom colour, with Opacity and Smooth edges. Bring forward and Send back stack overlapping areas, and Delete area removes one. Corner rounding, all water and nature rounds every water and nature shape at once.

Images I

Places a picture; drop files on the map to add several. Name, Width, Height (drag the corner handle; Alt+drag ignores the aspect ratio), Layer (below or above the map), Opacity, Corner radius, Border width, Border colour, Rotation °, X and Y. Smaller files work best: very large images make maps slow to save.

Timeline

The Timeline turns your map into an animation of the network being built, station by station and line by line, and exports it as a video. Switch to it with Timeline in the header. The preview is on the left, the settings on the right, and the track along the bottom.

Build the track

Generate from lines puts every line on the track in order, station by station. Generating replaces the current track. Add selected from map adds only what you had selected in the Map section, and Clear track starts over.

The track

  • Each card is one step: a station appearing, or a segment being drawn. Cards are grouped by line.
  • Drag cards, or whole groups by their coloured bar, to reorder them. A group's panel can move it before or after the next group or remove it.
  • Click a card for its Duration, seconds (or Use default duration), Move earlier, Move later, Insert pause after and Remove from track.
  • The track's header counts what's missing; Add missing puts it on the track, and Highlight missing marks it in the preview.

Playback

The bar under the preview: back to the start, previous item, play / pause, next item, and a slider to jump anywhere. Space plays and pauses; the arrow keys step; Del removes the selected card.

Timing

Draw segments: One by one, same time each or At a constant speed (with Speed, map units per second). Station, seconds, Segment, seconds, Hold at the end, seconds, and Keep drawing through stations on the same line.

Camera

Follow new stations and segments moves the view to what's being drawn, and Zoom while following sets how close it gets.

Line boards

Show a board about the line being drawn introduces each line in a corner of the picture. Default corner and Board size, % apply to every board; a line group's panel has its own Info board corner, a Second name and Extra lines of text.

End legend

Show the legend as a board at the end, with Legend position (same as the boards, or any of nine positions) and Legend size, %.

Audio

Add audio file… plays music under the animation and is included in the video; Remove audio takes it off.

Export video

Resolution (1280 × 720 or 1920 × 1080) and Export video (WebM). The video is made frame by frame with a progress bar (Cancel stops it). It needs Chrome, Edge or a recent Firefox.

Import and export

Export

  • PNG image…: choose the size (50% to 400%, or your own percentage); the picker shows the pixel size and warns when it's too big for the browser.
  • SVG vector: sharp at any size, editable in vector programs.
  • JSON backup file and Copy JSON: the whole map, to import later or share.

Import

  • Import JSON file… and Paste JSON… (maps menu): SNDT backups, and Rail Map Painter projects (in RMP: Download → Export project). Imports open as a new map.
  • Import from MTR…: reads a Minecraft Transit Railway System Map. On sndt.cc, enter the server's System Map address and press Fetch map. Anywhere else, open the address it gives you (/mtr/api/map/stations-and-routes), copy everything and paste it in.

Importing from MTR

  • Routes going both ways become one line. Routes far from the rest start unticked; possible variants of a route are marked but stay ticked.
  • Line types: tick which MTR route types to import and which SNDT line type each becomes.
  • Spread the map out (1× to 4×) gives crowded areas more room.
  • Import as a new map, or merge into the current one (undoable).

Maps and saving

Your maps save automatically in this browser; the status in the header shows when. The maps menu (the map's name) has New blank map, Duplicate this map, your other maps, and the imports. Delete a map from the same menu.

Back upMaps live in the browser you made them in. Clearing site data removes them, and they don't move to other devices by themselves. Export a JSON backup now and then.

Live rooms

Edit one map together in real time. Live room in the header → share this map live: SNDT makes a room code. Anyone with the code (or the invite link) can join and edit, no account needed. You see each other's cursors and names.

  • Copy code and Copy invite link share the room; In the room lists who's there.
  • The Activity log shows who changed what, and Map history can restore earlier versions.
  • Leave room keeps the map for you; the host's End room for everyone closes it.

Search

The search bar in the header (or Ctrl+K, or /) finds tools, actions (export, fit, tutorial, theme and more) and every map setting by name, including the words on a setting's buttons. Use ↑ ↓ and Enter, or click. Picking a setting opens its section, switches to Advanced if it needs to, and outlines the field for a moment. On phones, Search is in the ☰ menu.

Phones and the app

  • Drag with one finger to move around, pinch to zoom.
  • Double-tap something to select it, then drag it or its handles. Double-tap and drag draws a selection box. Select several in the bottom bar adds taps to the selection.
  • Long-press for a menu, for example to insert a station on a track.
  • The ☰ menu holds map settings, export, Map / Timeline, Simple / Advanced, search, help, language, theme, Ko-fi and Discord. The round button on the left opens the lines and stations lists.
  • Selecting something opens its settings in a sheet at the bottom; drag its bar up for everything.
  • While drawing, the bar at the bottom has Undo point, Done and cancel.

Install as an app

On sndt.cc, Install app (in the header or ☰ menu, when your browser offers it) adds SNDT to your home screen or desktop. It opens in its own window and works offline; your maps stay in that browser.

Troubleshooting

I can't find a setting

Type its name in the search bar. Many settings only show in Advanced mode.

A label won't sit where I want

Pick its side with the compass in the station's panel; it stays on that side. If Keep station names off lines is on and the side touches a line, the label moves further out on that side. In Advanced, drag the label or use Label nudge X and Y.

A segment ignores Mix types

It has its own bend. Select it and choose Use map default.

My map is gone

Maps are saved in the browser they were made in. Check the maps menu, use the same browser and device, and import a JSON backup if you have one.

Fetch map fails for my MTR server

Fetching works on sndt.cc. Elsewhere, open the server's /mtr/api/map/stations-and-routes address, copy everything and paste it into the dialog.
